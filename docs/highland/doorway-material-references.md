# High White Court doorway and lamp references

These photographs inform local materials and optics in the fictional court. The existing route, chooser and lighting interaction provide the same before and after views. The reference buildings and lamps serve as visual comparisons.

| Photograph | Detail used in this round |

The glass foot visibly overlaps the collar. The catalogue photograph is linked for visual review. |

The code uses the existing Rob Tuytel [White Plaster Rough 01 scan](../licenses/polyhaven-scans.md), adds a warm day sun angle and reflected light, and draws two rolled glass edges at the profile's actual ends. The chimney highlight responds to its surface normal and the direction of the sky and plaster opening. The small nook bounce is local. The photos are for review and are not runtime textures.

## Hall passage and near ridge

- Thiksey's warm, rocky shoulder divides into uneven spurs. Pale erosion channels separate the spurs and spread at their feet. Behind it, a gentler blue-gray ridge loses contrast in the air. [Thiksey rooftop photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Thikse_Gompa_-_View_from_rooftop.jpg), Ingo Mehling, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- The lip of the back gate catches daylight, while the inner plaster face, the bridge underside and the stone floor each fall into a different shade. The hall passage uses this separation to show depth through the small opening. The existing Hemis photo provides the wider court's cast-shadow and paving reference.
