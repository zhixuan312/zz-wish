## Approved storyline and setting

The same brick tower appears repeatedly between trees along the stream. The visitor passes its base and naturally faces the valley. They rest a small glass oil lamp on a worn stone table. At rest, the tower is behind them and the stream stays on the same side as during the walk. The visitor brings the wish written at Home. The place conveys the local custom and leaves the visitor's reason private.

## Camera acceptance

The brief sets the 30 s walk. The shared staging reference puts the opening at 26–30 s. Valley's exact trees, crossing time and poses remain review proposals. Use an eye-level route with clearance through the gate and around the plinth. Measure peak rotation on the eased trace against the 25°/s common review target documented for Bell Lane in GOALS. After the chooser, aim to keep the same gaze and lens through lighting, using at most 0.15 m eye-height change and 5° of aim change as conservative settle targets from the [runtime camera guidance](../../web/src/engine/scene.js#L2665). GOALS lists separate exceptions for Snow Roof and for the phone Draw sheet.

The [scene runtime](../../web/src/engine/scene.js#L2025) pulls procedural phone poses back 1.45×, drops their authored FOV, and [lowers the chooser aim](../../web/src/engine/scene.js#L2077). Valley does not have authored phone poses. The [camera trace](../../web/src/engine/scene.js#L1941), [walk progress](../../web/src/engine/scene.js#L2441) and [realism capture harness](../../tests/tier2/realism.mjs) can verify position, gaze, timing and UI coverage. Capture the whole path at actual speed with no Skip, then a separate Skip run. Judge geometry and framing with the UI visible as well as in clean frames.

## Six local times

[The shared clock](../../shared/dayPart.ts#L4) names six periods. Valley supplies a day palette and a night palette in its [descriptor](../../web/src/engine/places/valley-pagoda.js#L4). [The renderer](../../web/src/engine/scene.js#L1520) interpolates exposure, fog, sky and light from local time, with no Valley-specific `lightAt` or six-part audio. Each row identifies a source-driven difference and what still needs checking. Times are proposed review samples in local time.

| Period | Implemented in shared lighting | Valley runtime check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dawn, 06:00 | Warm sunrise, blue lift and elevated haze. | Tower can be found through trees and the path is readable through mist. |
| Morning, 09:00 | Day palette, rising light, residual haze. | Tree gaps, bank and brick remain distinct. |
| Midday, 13:00 | Highest daytime light key and reduced haze. | Water path, stone contact and valley depth read under the brightest look. |
| Afternoon, 16:00 | Lower, warmer daylight. | Shadows leave enough contrast at the gate and slab. |
| Dusk, 18:00 | Warm horizon with a day/night mix and shared lamplight. | Ordinary lamp and visitor lamp are distinct and the stream stays legible. |
| Night, 22:00 | Night palette, moon direction and shared fill. | Entrance, tree gaps, plinth, water, stone and glass are readable. Historical captures show brightness improvements and the unresolved framing. |

The World Bible calls for distinct light, color, sound and mood in each period, with smooth transitions. Verify all six at entrance and at rest, sample the transitions during a live burn, and assess local sound when authored. Midday and night bracket visibility for the first visual change.
