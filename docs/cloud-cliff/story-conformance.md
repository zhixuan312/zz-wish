Baseline: `fbe82e3` on `s5/cloud-cliff`. Approved sources live in the shared One Light directory: [Ten Place Storylines](../../../ten-place-storylines.md) (Cloud Cliff Court row, approved November 21, 2026), [World Bible](../../../world-bible.md) (rules for every place, Home and Returning), [Experience and Music Brief](../../../one-light-experience-and-music-brief.md) (§§2, 5–7), and [Goals](../../../GOALS.md) (current roster and production rules). The Cloud row defines the local story. The common rules apply throughout. The Snow Roof and Pine Gate descriptions supply no Cloud-specific customs, geography, music or season.

**Matches** means the stated part is implemented in the inspected source. **Differs** means related behavior exists but falls short of the approved experience. **Missing** means the Cloud-specific implementation is absent. The runtime checks below are pending for the playable court. Existing cover evidence applies to its original fixed views.

## First proposed scene change

Create a review-only route preview with an inner wall, a firm shoulder and the existing valley where geometry coverage allows. Place the lantern at the endpoint as a scale and framing reference. The scene data and sampler would live in `web/src/engine/places/cloud-cliff-route.js`, with a standalone fixture at `tests/visual/cloud-cliff-route.mjs`. The preview should keep a stable geometry baseline so a camera or coverage problem can be judged before any volume, baking or ignition work.

Review the same plan and continuous walk at **1440×900** and **390×844**. Capture day and night at the entrance, wall-side approach (about 12 s), just before rounding the shoulder (about 22 s), the first open view (about 26 s), and the final grounded hold (30 s). Show that the valley stays largely occluded on approach, ground remains visible and continuous through the turn, and the opposite face gives the release a readable depth cue. Verify camera trace limits, framing, and endpoint parity for skip and reduced motion. The timing marks are proposed review beats for Cloud.

The first scene-local change can use the existing camera metric helper and shipped asset paths in the standalone fixture. No production hook change is needed for that review. For integration, reuse `viewPhone`, `walkSeconds`, `createWalk`, and camera tracing in `scene.js`. Agree with Boss on Home and playable registry identity, chooser visibility and prefetch routing, and any opt-in post-walk settle hook. Later ignition work needs an opt-in place lifecycle for catch opening, flame catch, closure, cancellation and endings. The cover's stationary lamp spill and fixed-view assets need separate validation before they are used for a playable lamp.

This audit used source and shipped asset inspection. No new desktop or phone capture was taken, and no build or GPU job was run.
