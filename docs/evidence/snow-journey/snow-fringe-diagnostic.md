# Snow-bank edge contribution controls

The bounded hidden-texel padding test substantially reduced the dark bank fringe at both sizes. The sampling-domain repair is selected for production assembly; the unpruned diagnostic itself is not a delivery asset. All images below are private diagnostics; no public assets, original bakes or accepted core snapshot were changed by these comparisons. Six time looks remain held until the complete corrected package is ready.

The [normal optical comparison](../../../artifacts/snow-geometry-ab/float32-position/comparison.html) contains eight views: current optimized 16-bit positions and genuine authored FLOAT32 positions, desktop/phone walk-2/3. Actual materials, embedded image payloads, all 12 lightmaps/decode parameters and original scene source match. The FP32 candidate is unpruned diagnostic geometry, not final adoption. Every image exactly reuses the completed core's camera world/projection matrices. Particles are hidden and physical/flame clocks held consistently. Precision slightly reduces jaggedness but does not remove the fringe.

The separate [filtering/ownership controls](../../../artifacts/snow-geometry-ab/float32-filter-ownership/comparison.html) contain eight FP32 views. Nearest min/mag filtering with base-level-only lookup retains hard dark segments, so mip choice alone is insufficient. Flat surface IDs show continuous bank/ground coverage without a black hole. This does not establish that the contact surfaces never overlap or that their baked texels are correct. The original sampler is restored before surface-ID rendering.

The final [contribution controls](../../../artifacts/snow-geometry-ab/float32-contributions/comparison.html) contain two desktop walk-3 stills, with the exact same camera and normal samplers/exposure:

| [White baked diffuse](../../../artifacts/snow-geometry-ab/float32-contributions/float32/desktop-en/walk-3-white-baked.png) | Existing baked shader retained; white diffuse, no UV0 map/normal map/vertex tint; roughness 1, metalness 0; specular/emission output removed | Dark fringe remains |
| [Unlit original albedo](../../../artifacts/snow-geometry-ab/float32-contributions/float32/desktop-en/walk-3-unlit-albedo.png) | Original map/color/vertex tint and alpha, without lightmap/normal/specular; original fog/tone-mapping flags retained | Dark fringe disappears |

This isolates the visible strip to the baked-lighting/shaded contribution rather than original UV0 albedo or vertex tint. It does not yet distinguish the authored contact geometry, baked field and log-domain interpolation mechanisms under CPU tracing.

All three runs completed with zero page/console/HTTP errors, unchanged source/assets during capture and exact camera checks. Controls restored original materials/samplers before closure. Reports retain control definitions, exposure, texture format/filter metadata, surface color mappings and exact matrix references. The accepted 88-image/42-sequence core and all real saved records remain preserved.

The subsequent [certified contact-padding comparison](../../../artifacts/snow-geometry-ab/contact-padding/comparison.html) adds eight standard-sampler views at both sizes. Its F32 GLB, materials/images, exposure, camera matrices and ten unaffected maps are identical. Only court-night and hall-night KTX payloads differ. The receipt and independent proof certify 2,098 court plus 682 hall hidden base recipients, using visible donors from the same chart/owner within eight pixels; visible/uncertain base float RGBA bits and original masters are unchanged. Lossy KTX output remains a separate optical check. [Exact receipt/proof hashes and pair checks](contact-padding-summary.json) are retained.

In the desktop and phone walk-3 comparisons, the hard black outline is substantially reduced to a softer blue-gray contact edge. This directly supports filtering of buried near-black texels as a material contributor. Independent review found no broad bright halo, shifted silhouette or changed step detail. A faint grey/scalloped seam remains, and the matched native Metal crop blends the joins more smoothly. This is a reduction of sampling artifacts, not complete removal. The run had zero errors and all sources stayed unchanged; both original and padded diagnostic snapshots remain preserved.

The matching native reference is `art/build/snow-roof-fringe-native-held/held-walk3-lower-oidn.png`, rendered with the held runtime camera, accepted night lighting and visitor light off: eight Cycles samples on Metal with OIDN. It has a smooth join. Receiver-ray analysis finds four of five dark sample centres geometrically buried in the other snow surface, while all five bright control centres are clear. Most dark energy was already present in the noisy EXR; denoising and compression alone do not explain it. The certified mask changes hidden sample centres only, using same-chart, same-owner visible donors within eight pixels. No radiance threshold selects a recipient or donor.
