# Remaining visual limits

These are observations from the complete corrected night journey and exact-camera snow-contact comparisons. They are separate from UI, record-settlement and frame-budget checks. The [final review](final-review.md) links the actual corrected-package evidence.

- The exterior trees have straight trunks and repeated thin branch patterns. The sparse distant roofs and nearly level horizon still read as a constructed scene. The footprint track is subtle in the wide night view.
- The small glowing red shade is clean and saturated enough to read as a graphic object. The close room composition crops much of the scanned altar and screen, while the final burning view faces back through the doors. Those assets add detail during the walk and ignition, but they do not fill every held screen.
- Brass highlights show some faceting and a smooth, plastic quality in diagnostic close views. Incense ash and nearby small surfaces have less convincing detail than the photographed wood and lacquer props.
- The snow-contact correction substantially reduces the harsh dark outline at both sizes. A faint grey/scalloped seam remains; the matched native Cycles image blends the join more smoothly. Phone walk-3 crops most of the outer bank bases, so that frame alone cannot prove their full condition.
- The baked payload is large. Lazy map loading reduces initial requests, but the delivered court is not an eight-megabyte scene.

The scene is more detailed and temporally stable, but the captures do not justify describing every moment as indistinguishable from filmed footage. M1 phone viewport/touch emulation also does not establish physical-phone performance. Audible listening, a real-duration endurance burn and assistive-technology use have not been claimed.
