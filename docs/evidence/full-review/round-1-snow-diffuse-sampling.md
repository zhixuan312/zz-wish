# Snow D7: diffuse sampling diagnosis

The adaptive Source17 package is **not visually accepted**. Its actual final application frame still shows mottled carrying beams and structured bright column patches. Budget and physical-contact proofs remain valid observations, but do not establish optical quality.

The read-only comparison in [the numeric receipt](round-1-snow-diffuse-sampling.json) samples the same 38 decoded UV positions and all relevant chart cores/interiors from the actual 8/32/64/256 raw and denoised EXRs. The carrying-beam chart has 2,096 core texels. Its raw high-pass standard deviation falls from 0.001286 at 8 to 0.000330 at 64, then rises to 0.000390 at the configured 256 maximum. The near-column chart loses fine raw noise, while its denoised high-pass deviation rises from 0.000289 at 32 to 0.000688 at 256. At held pixel (490,350), raw 256 red is 0.006331 and denoised red is 0.006389: that broad patch already exists before OIDN.

Actual saved settings expose a sampling qualification. Adaptive sampling is enabled, threshold is 0.01, minimum is automatic, film exposure is 1, and there is no time limit or sample subset. At the installed Blender build `02f0899d78e3`, the backend derives an automatic minimum of 64 and permits stopping after that. Thus the configured 256 maximum does not prove 256 samples at every surface texel. The 64 budget cannot stop early below its automatic minimum, but a per-texel sample-count pass was not retained. This follows the actual [integrator minimum calculation](https://github.com/blender/blender/blob/02f0899d78e3/intern/cycles/scene/integrator.cpp#L436-L487) and the [bake kernel’s convergence check](https://github.com/blender/blender/blob/02f0899d78e3/intern/cycles/kernel/integrator/init_from_bake.h#L104-L111).

The stopping test uses film exposure, rather than the native view EV. In dark regions its normalization is approximately the square root of intensity; uniformly dividing radiance by 40 can therefore reduce the stopping error by about √40, apart from its epsilon. This is a reason to test fixed sampling, not proof of the realized counts. The exact [backend error formula](https://github.com/blender/blender/blob/02f0899d78e3/intern/cycles/kernel/film/adaptive_sampling.h#L50-L75) and hashed source snapshots accompany the receipt.

The earlier smooth native beauty is also an unmatched reference: it uses maximum/diffuse/glossy/transmission/transparent bounce limits 4/2/2/2/6, whereas the bake uses 6/4/2/12/4. No conclusion that the residual pattern is solely OIDN, caustics, codec or source geometry follows from that comparison.

The next bounded diagnostic uses the same immutable Source17 physical scene with adaptive and animated-seed sampling disabled. Its new fingerprint is `4f4b6a7a`; exact JSON physical inputs differ from the old payload only in cache policy and integrator settings. Four actual cache mutations invalidate that fingerprint, while changing only the sample budget from 64 to 256 preserves it. The new hall/night fixed-256 result must be compared with an integrator-matched native beauty before any further complete bake or delivery adoption.

The selected image peaks are biased toward the eight-sample failure. Full-chart statistics reduce that selection bias but include real illumination gradients and denoiser structure. No result here establishes reference-quality convergence, film photorealism or total blotch removal.
