# One Light: approved place storylines
Owner approved on 2026-11-21. Preserve the ten existing names, order and vessel types. Players bring the wish they wrote at Home. The place explains the local custom and leaves their private reason open. The world bible and experience brief govern the shared journey and the two established places.

| Place | Approach and discovery | Lighting and staying |
|---|---|---|
| Pine Gate Steps | Hear water under the pines. Across the double-pine crest the terraces open; a bamboo pipe feeds the spring basin beside a porter's resting place. | Take a basket candle, tie the wish cloth below the stand and light it in the stone niche. Near spring water and distant flooded fields share the view. |
| Bamboo Rain Cloister | Walk through wet bamboo into shelter. Rain shifts overhead. A cleared drain keeps the inner shelf dry. | Fill and light a celadon oil cup inside the dry line. Water drips from tile to channel beyond the steady lamp. |
| Cloud Cliff Court | Follow the inner rock wall. Rounding the shoulder reveals the depth of the valley from firm ground. | Open the worn brass catch, light the candle and close the storm lantern. Passing cloud uncovers the opposite rock face. |
| Lotus Pond Court | Follow the pond wall onto the bridge. See the old lamp stands reflected before rounding the rail to see them directly. | Set the lotus candle in the stone seat above open water. A ripple breaks its reflection and the reflected flame gathers again. |
| Tide Temple | Hear surf in a lane facing away from the sea. Beyond the granite wall the harbour entrance opens; salt marks its exposed side. | Place the hooded lamp in the sheltered stone recess. Watch the harbour mouth between waves; the story leaves the wait open. |
| Snow Roof Court | Two warm gate lamps guide the snow walk. Enter the swept court, see the peak and the signs of a tended shelter. | Take a red gauze lantern, tie the wooden tag and hang it from the centre cord on the single hall beam. The half-open door still shows blowing snow. |
| Loess Cliff Court | Enter a sunken cave court from a cart lane. Side light picks out the rounded window ledge and the stain of a lamp repeatedly set down. | Fill the shallow clay lamp, tend the wick and set it back. Light reveals layers in the earthen window; the empty cup remains after burning down. |
| High White Court | Wind crosses the thick wall. A deep doorway leads to a quiet open court under a wide sky. | Lift the chimney, fill the brass lamp, light it and settle the glass back. Hear the glass meet brass; grass above the wall still bends in the wind. |
| Lane's End Bell Court | Pass a familiar shop window. At the end of the eaves a small door frames a tree shadow. Through it, discover the old tree, low bell pavilion and inner lamp rack. | Take a lattice lantern, select an oil cup, tie the wish tag, light with a taper and set it among older lamps. From the resting place recognize the same shop window. Street sounds continue more softly through the door. |
| Valley Pagoda Court | The brick tower appears repeatedly through trees along the stream. Pass its base and naturally face the valley. | Rest the small glass oil lamp on the worn stone table. The tower is behind the visitor and the stream remains on the same side as on the walk. |

## Bell Lane: first production round
Retain the existing street and court geometry where it serves the story. Map the shop window, threshold, tree, bell pavilion and lamp rack on one plan. The 30-second walk follows the shop eaves (0-12 s), narrows toward the tree shadow (12-22 s), crosses the small door at eye level (22-26 s), and holds the open court (26-30 s). At rest, the near flame and the original window must read together on desktop and phone. Place the source of street sound on the street and soften it across the wall. The bell is occasional and motivated. Use six local times: sweeping and opening shutters at dawn, open shops in morning, short eave shadows at midday, tree shadow on the bell base in afternoon, shutters and first window lights at dusk, a legible way home at night. Natural exhaustion leaves a dark lattice and tag; early extinction leaves oil. Verify against the common camera limits and both viewports. First deliver a plan, day/night entrance, threshold and seated frames, plus a continuous walk before detail polish.
