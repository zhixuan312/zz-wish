# Snow Roof Court story conformance

The current court fails the reveal, hall readability and lived-in story. At 13 seconds the camera is already under the gate. At 27 seconds it is inside the hall facing the gilded screen. The daytime and nighttime burning frames make the lantern readable, but leave most of the occupied interior too dark to read. The rebuilt walk must cross the pass and arrive at the open court before the player goes inside to choose a lantern.
