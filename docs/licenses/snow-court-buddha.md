# Snow Roof Court seated marble figure

Daniel Pett photographed the early eighth-century seated marble Buddha held by the Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art, Freer Gallery of Art, accession F1917.243. Pett published this independent photogrammetry from 117 photographs under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The museum collection record is the source for the object history and material. Its photographs are separate from Pett's scan. [Museum collection record](https://asia.si.edu/explore-art-culture/collections/search/edanmdm%3Afsg_F1917.243/) · [Pett's CC0 model record](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/seated-figure-of-buddha-a4d22eb6d3c44848bd49b89fb4c28759) · [NLM dataset catalog](https://datasetcatalog.nlm.nih.gov/dataset?q=0001912921).

The source is the [public Objaverse GLB](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse/resolve/main/glbs/000-067/a4d22eb6d3c44848bd49b89fb4c28759.glb), which embeds Pett's name, the original model URL and the CC0 dedication in its glTF asset metadata. The source GLB is 11,193,208 bytes with SHA-256 `5834c2c7fc67c92b3bb258dc0aa5393bd79fd529358bfd33360bfeebae3335e7`.

The local derivative at `art/assets/snow-court/seated-marble-scan.npz` trims scan fragments and the uneven underside, cuts a flat bearing face, and fits the existing altar. Simplification preserves the carved face, hair and robes. The photograph atlas provides the vertex albedo after neutral colour correction. The scan's matte roughness and zero metallic factor are retained. Independent lightmap charts carry the current hall illumination. The figure's original wrist losses remain visible. The scene uses the figure and its stone seat without museum display furniture, text or temple architecture.
